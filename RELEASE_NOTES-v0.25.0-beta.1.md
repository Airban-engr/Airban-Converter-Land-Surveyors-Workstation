# Airban Converter v0.25.0-beta.1

## Added

- Calculation Audit output tab in the Parcel Workstation.
- Calculation Audit CSV export through the active parcel CSV button.
- Calculation Audit sheet in the Excel-compatible survey workbook.
- Calculation Audit section in the branded survey report.
- Calculation Audit section in the Survey Package Manifest.

## Calculation Audit Contents

- Course number, from beacon, and to beacon.
- Start and end coordinates for each boundary course.
- Delta easting and delta northing.
- Computed bearing and distance.
- `Y*dX`, `X*dY`, signed double-area contribution, and signed area contribution for shoelace-area review.

## Notes

- This release strengthens reviewability. It does not replace professional judgement, field-note checks, cadastral authority requirements, or independent control validation.
