# Airban Converter v0.16.0-beta.1

This beta improves the professional export package for **The Land Surveyors Workstation**.

## Added

- Computation Summary worksheet at the front of the Excel-compatible survey workbook.
- Computation Summary section in the branded HTML survey report.
- Summary evidence for project details, coordinate reference, transformation set, native and working units, beacon count, perimeter, area, traverse evidence, and Quality Control status.
- Consistent generated timestamp across workbook summary and History of Survey sheets.
- Beta guide, deployment checklist, README, and feedback template updates for summary export testing.

## Notes For Testers

- Open the workbook first sheet and confirm it reads like a useful cover page for the computation package.
- In the branded report, confirm the Computation Summary appears immediately after Project Summary.
- Confirm the summary reflects whether traverse reduction and Bowditch adjustment evidence were attached before export.
